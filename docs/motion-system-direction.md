# Motion system — where it evolves next (direction, not a task)

Nitish, 17 Oct 2026, after Variant 2 (the Drivetrain port) and its first fixes. Not a next move yet: a direction to
check every later change against, "whenever we feel there is a need or we can start doing it".

## What he said

- Variant 2 is better than before, but it still reads general and generic: timing, easing and consistency sound like
  things everybody knows, or like a part of any design system or of how a product works. It doesn't yet make the case
  that motion needs a system of its own.
- It is far more than basic knowledge, and the page should feel that way.
- Evolve it towards what the system makes possible: the exceptional, rare things found in apps today, and how
  persistently and beautifully the apps we love carry them out. That care is the reason people love those apps more
  than apps where it is done badly or missing.

## What that means when we act on it (our reading, to confirm with him)

- **The hygiene stays as the floor, not the headline.** Right durations, easing and feedback are where the page
  starts, not what it argues for.
- **Show what only a system can carry.** For example:
  - continuity across screens: the thing you tapped stays the same thing wherever it goes;
  - motion you can interrupt and steer with a gesture: it follows the finger, reverses midway, keeps its velocity;
  - states that live through uncertainty, like V1's Pay contract (pending, checking, done, failed);
  - a spatial model, so that navigation has a map;
  - signature moments kept for the pivotal few.
- **Persistence is the point.** These moments hold on every screen and through every release, not once in a showcase.
  That is what a system gives that a one-off animation can't.
- **Make the contrast with and without.** Keep the with/without device, but aim it at these rarer behaviours as well
  as at the basics.
- **Build on what Variant 1 already has.** The container transform, the Pay contract, the developer's view and reduced
  motion can feed into this.

Rules that still apply: one change at a time; 04's greys; no Cars24 names; headings say what we're talking about;
each section states an idea, then shows the example that proves it.
